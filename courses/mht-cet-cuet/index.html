MHT-CET PCM: Target 99.5+ Percentile for Top State Colleges

MHT-CET is a zero-negative-marking exam where speed and textbook line-level familiarity dictate the outcome. 80% weightage is derived from the Class 12th Maharashtra State Board syllabus and 20% from Class 11th topics.

Exam Pattern

MHT-CET PCM Structure

  • Mathematics (100 Marks): 50 Questions (2 Marks each). Time: 90 Minutes. High weightage on Calculus, Vectors, 3D, and Trigonometry.
  • Physics & Chemistry (100 Marks): 50 Physics + 50 Chemistry Questions (1 Mark each). Time: 90 Minutes. No negative marking.
  • Computer-Based Test (CBT): Regular weekly full-length CBT tests in our computer facility to simulate the exact CET interface.

MHT-CET PCB: Pharmacy, Biotech & Agri Streams

Designed for students targeting B.Pharm, Pharm.D, and agricultural universities in Maharashtra. Complete alignment with Maharashtra State Board Biology, Physics, and Chemistry.

Biology Dominance (100 Marks)

100 multiple-choice questions (1 Mark each) in 90 minutes. Rapid-fire recall drilling to finish Biology in 50 minutes, leaving ample review time.

CUET UG (Common University Entrance Test)

Single-window examination for admission into prestigious central universities (Delhi University, BHU, JNU, Jamia Millia Islamia) across Science and General streams.

Domain Subject + General Test Modules

Preparation for Section IA/IB (Language), Section II (Domain Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology), and Section III (General Test: Numerical Ability, Reasoning, Current Affairs).

Subject Syllabi

MHT-CET & CUET Syllabus Details
