Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 · Last Updated: Academic Session 2025-26

1. Commitment to Student Privacy

Kota Rankers Point ("KRP", "we", "us", or "our"), located at 3rd Floor, Navya Business Park, Manaji Nagar, Narhe, Pune – 411041, is committed to safeguarding the personal information of our students, parents, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data collection and handling practices.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information solely when voluntarily submitted through our admission enquiry forms, contact forms, WhatsApp communications, or in-person campus visits. This information includes:

  • Student full name, age, and current academic class or stream.
  • Contact telephone number and WhatsApp mobile number.
  • Parent or guardian name and contact coordinates.
  • Email address and residential city/town.
  • Course interests and hostel accommodation preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for legitimate academic and administrative purposes:

  • To respond to your admission enquiries and schedule diagnostic counselling sessions.
  • To share course brochures, fee schedules, and scholarship test details via WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  • To process hostel room allocation and academic batch registration.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your contact details to third-party commercial marketing agencies or lead aggregators.

4. Data Storage & Static Architecture

Our website operates on a 100% static architecture without server-side database tracking. When you submit an admission form, data is processed locally in your browser to format direct communication via WhatsApp or email. No credit card, net banking, or financial credentials are ever requested or stored on this website.

5. Student Ranks & Testimonials Policy

Student photographs, ranks, selections, and testimonials displayed on krppune.com are published strictly with the formal consent of the student and their parent or guardian. If a student or parent wishes to modify or withdraw a published testimonial, they may notify our administration office at any time.
