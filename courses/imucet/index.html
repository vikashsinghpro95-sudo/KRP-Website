Exam Blueprint

IMU-CET Written Syllabus Coverage (200 Questions)

Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work Energy Power, Rotational Dynamics, Gravitation, Thermodynamics, Electrostatics, Magnetism, Optics, and Modern Physics at 10+2 level.
Calculus, Vectors, 3D Geometry, Probability, Trigonometry, Matrices, Coordinate Geometry, and high-speed computational shortcuts.
Basic Chemical Concepts, Atomic Structure, Periodic Classification, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Thermodynamics, and Organic Basics.
Vocabulary, sentence correction, idioms, verbal reasoning, quantitative aptitude, data interpretation, and basic maritime awareness.
