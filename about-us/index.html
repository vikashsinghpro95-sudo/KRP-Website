Our Story

Why Kota Rankers Point Was Established

For years, students in Pune and surrounding districts who wanted to clear exams like IIT-JEE, NEET, or NDA faced a difficult choice: leave home for Rajasthan or enroll in local coaching classes where individualized care and structured discipline were rarely maintained.

Located in Manaji Nagar, Narhe, Pune, Kota Rankers Point brought the complete Kota ecosystem directly to Pune: structured sheets, rigorous daily practice problems (DPP), micro-level doubt counters, and regular national-level testing.

Recognizing the unique discipline required for armed forces examinations (NDA, CDS, AFCAT, Naval Entry), we expanded to include comprehensive SSB interview preparation and full residential boarding for boys and girls.
