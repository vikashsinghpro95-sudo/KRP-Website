Pure Science Research

IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) Coaching

For students passionate about fundamental scientific research, biotechnology, astronomy, and physics. The IAT is the gateway to the 7 premier Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research (IISER Pune, Kolkata, Mohali, Bhopal, Thiruvananthapuram, Tirupati, Berhampur) and IISc Bangalore.

IAT tests Physics (15 Qs), Chemistry (15 Qs), Mathematics (15 Qs), and Biology (15 Qs). Our faculty guides PCM students through essential Biology concepts and PCB students through fundamental Mathematics to maximize total score.
Air Force Officer Entry

AFCAT (Air Force Common Admission Test)

Targeted coaching for graduate aspirants aiming for Flying Branch, Ground Duty (Technical), and Ground Duty (Non-Technical) commissions in the Indian Air Force.

  • • General Awareness: Defence history, polity, geography, national current affairs
  • • Verbal Ability in English: Comprehension, error detection, sentence completion
  • • Numerical Ability: High-speed arithmetic, percentages, profit-loss, time-speed-distance
  • • Reasoning & Military Aptitude: Spatial ability, verbal & non-verbal rotation
