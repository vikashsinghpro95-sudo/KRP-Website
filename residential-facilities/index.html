Boys' Residential Wing

Located in Manaji Nagar, Narhe, Pune · Walking distance to classrooms

Disciplined Routine & Peer Focus

The Boys' Residence is tailored for high-intensity competitive preparation. With resident faculty on campus, students adhere to morning physical drills (for defence aspirants), scheduled evening silent self-study from 6:30 PM to 9:30 PM, and compulsory lights-out at 11:00 PM.

Verified Facilities & Amenities

Individual Bed & Wardrobe
Ergonomic Study Desks
Resident Male Warden
Hygienic Balanced Mess
Controlled Gate Access
Power Backup & RO Water

House Rules & Supervision:

  • • Mandatory attendance during evening self-study halls.
  • • Strict gate check-in by 8:30 PM unless attending scheduled academic lectures.
  • • Weekly parent attendance and mock test score reporting.
  • • Zero tolerance for indiscipline, bullying, or disruption of study environment.
Safe & Independent Facility

Girls' Residential Wing

Secure dedicated building in Narhe, Pune · Strict perimeter safety

Safety, Dignity & Academic Peace

The Girls' Residence operates under strict perimeter security with 24/7 resident female wardens. Female cadets preparing for NDA, MNS, JEE, and NEET enjoy an encouraging, peaceful atmosphere with high-speed internet for mock test reviews and healthy meals.

Verified Facilities & Security

24/7 Female Warden
Biometric Entry Access
Quiet Personal Study Desks
Dedicated Kitchen & Dining
First Aid & Doctor On-Call
Direct Parent Reporting

Parent Safety Protocol:

  • • Strict gate closing at 8:00 PM; no night outs without written parent authorization.
  • • Male visitors strictly prohibited beyond reception area.
  • • 24-hour warden mobile contact line provided to parents.
  • • Emergency vehicle standby and proximity to Sinhgad Medical College.
