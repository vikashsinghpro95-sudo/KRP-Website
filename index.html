Integrated Classroom Programme

Board Preparation + Competitive Exam Preparation

Kota Rankers Point (KRP) provides integrated classroom programmes that combine school board preparation with competitive examination preparation for NDA, SSB, IIT-JEE, NEET, IMUCET and MHT-CET (PCM/B).

From Foundation Classes (8th–10th) and Classes 11th–12th to NDA written examination and SSB interview preparation, IIT-JEE Main & Advanced, NEET-UG, IMUCET, MHT-CET, IISER, CUET, Olympiads, Homi Bhabha, NTSE and MTSE, KRP helps students strengthen concepts, practise systematically and prepare for their target examinations. Our teaching team includes experienced educators and subject specialists, including faculty with doctoral (PhD), engineering and research backgrounds.

01

Board Syllabus Coverage

Comprehensive board syllabus coverage seamlessly integrated alongside relevant competitive-exam concepts and analytical depth.

02

Concept-Building & Guided Solving

Structured concept-building step by step with guided problem-solving to ensure students never memorize formulas blindly.

03

Practice Questions, Revision & Mock Tests

Graded daily practice questions, scheduled topic-wise revision, and full-length exam mock tests calibrated per programme.

04

Progress Review & Guidance

Continuous individual performance tracking with structured feedback on specific areas requiring conceptual improvement.

Priority Defence Wing

Prepare for Defence Examinations and the SSB Interview

KRP gives priority to NDA and SSB preparation. The programme includes NDA written-examination preparation, Ground School subjects and SSB interview preparation. SSB-focused practice may include Group Discussion (GD), Personal Interview (PI), communication practice, current-affairs discussions, officer-like qualities awareness and structured practice for relevant SSB tasks, according to the programme offered.

NDA Written & Ground School Subjects

Thorough syllabus coverage for Mathematics and General Ability Test (GAT) alongside aviation and military ground school essentials.

Group Discussion (GD) & Personal Interview (PI)

Rigorous communication training, current affairs analysis, lecturette delivery, and psychology interview grooming.

Officer-Like Qualities (OLQ) Awareness & Tasks

Structured practice for GTO ground obstacles, situational reaction tests (SRT), and building leadership discipline.
